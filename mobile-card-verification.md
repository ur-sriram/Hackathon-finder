# Mobile source-card verification

The development preview was captured at **390 × 844** after the source-specific card update. The opportunity cards stack into a single-column layout, long titles and descriptions wrap, metadata pills break onto multiple lines, and the source action remains inside the card width. No horizontal overflow or clipped card content was observed in the full-page mobile capture. This complements the render-level test covering long descriptions, missing fields, structured eligibility, tags, and prize payloads.
